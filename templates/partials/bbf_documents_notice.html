BBF Claims — Required Supporting Documents

Member's Documents

  • ✓ Copy of Payslip
  • ✓ Copy of National ID

Deceased's Documents

  • ✓ Burial Permit
  • ✓ Copy of ID, Birth Certificate, or Birth Notification

Proof of Relationship

  • ✓ Evidence showing relationship between the member and the deceased

Institutional Confirmation

  • ✓ Introduction Letter from the Head of Institution
